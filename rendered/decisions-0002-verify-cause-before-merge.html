0002 · Verify the cause before merging reports

owner Mateusz Jakowlew accepted reviewed 2026-10-28 · 0 days ago

Date: 28 October 2026 · Signed by: Mateusz Jakowlew / Codex

Supersedes: 0001. The earlier accepted record is preserved unchanged.

Context

The review of #4471 finds distinct parser and booking-v3 hint causes inside one ticket. Subject matching hides this difference; browser-derived intake tags also disagree with the tenant-based evidence. The desk handles multiple products, so a booking classifier cannot become a universal rule.

Decision

Use subject similarity to find candidates, then read each report. Verify the product and the observed behavior, look up its tenant and configuration, and compare evidence for the cause. For these date reports, inspect tenant locale, channel, form version and the actual input/output dates. Merge only when the evidence supports the same cause. Give distinct causes separate tickets, and retain uncertain reports for investigation.

Count distinct report IDs. Attach intake labels as context, not proof. Read received and merged timestamps separately. Rebuild from a current export when the queue changes.

Consequences

Triage takes more reading. Engineering receives independently actionable problems with auditable counts. Ambiguous reports stay visible. Each product needs its own evidence; the booking-specific classifier is a helper, not a universal diagnosis.
